# Content Writing Studio

Turns generic, stiff AI drafts into sharp, high-retention human writing in your own voice — combining **Trend/CRO Research → Viral Hooks → Storytelling → Dumbify → Anti-AI & Voice DNA**.

---

## 5-Stage Writing Chain

1. **Trend & Conversion Brief** *(optional for growth/offers)*: Ground hooks in real 30-day community discussions (Reddit, X, YouTube, HN) and clear above-the-fold value propositions.
2. **Viral Hook (0–2 s / Line 1)**: Deliver **Topic Clarity** + **On-Target Curiosity** immediately; eliminate throat-clearing and vague openers.
3. **Storytelling Body**: Hold attention with **tension, turns, open loops, varied sentence cadence, and a clean payoff** (not dry bullet lists).
4. **Dumbify (Clarity Pass)**: Lower reading level to **~8th grade (body) / ~6th grade (hooks)** without dumbing down the insight.
5. **Anti-AI & Voice DNA Filter**: Strip structural AI tells (*Staging, Rhythm by Rule, Inflation, Formatting by Rule, Leftovers, Wrong Reader*) and match your real spoken Voice DNA.

## Mandatory Self-Audit & Revise-Before-Delivery Gate

Before delivering any final script, post, or copy:
1. Run the 3-pass Anti-AI & Dumbify audit (`references/dumbify-and-anti-ai.md`) and read aloud to confirm natural spoken rhythm.
2. Verify zero lost or altered facts, numbers, names, or causal claims; revise any stiff/generic line before presenting.
