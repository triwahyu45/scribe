# Viral Hooks, Storytelling & 30-Day Trend/CRO

---

## 1. Viral Hooks (`viral-hooks`)

A hook has ONE job: help the right viewer decide to keep watching/reading in **1–2 seconds**. It must deliver both:
1. **Topic Clarity**: Immediately clear what this is about.
2. **On-Target Curiosity**: Clear who it's for + an open question pulling them forward.

### 4 Hook Killers to Cut Immediately
- **Throat-clearing**: *"In today's video I'm going to show you..."*, *"Have you ever wondered..."*
- **Vague curiosity**: *"This one trick changed everything"* (without stating what domain/outcome).
- **Over-complexity**: Multi-clause openers above a 6th-grade reading level.
- **Wrong target**: Broad openers that attract everyone and hook no one.

---

## 2. Storytelling (`storytelling`)

The hook earns 2 seconds; **storytelling earns the rest**:
- **Tension & Turn**: Start in motion or conflict, escalate stakes, pivot with a concrete realization (*"but then..."*), and land a clear **payoff**.
- **Open Loops**: Plant an unanswered question early and close it near the end.
- **Rhythm & Dance**: Alternate short punchy 3–6 word lines with medium explanatory lines — never write uniform robotic paragraphs.
- **Show, Don't List**: Turn tips into lived moments, specific numbers, or mini-scenes.

## 2.5. 5-Act Engineering & Quantitative Documentary Formula (`110–125 WPM`)

Use this calm, high-authority narrative arc for technical, STEM, energy, and engineering explainers:

1. **Numeric / Physical Contrast Hook (0–5s):** Lead with a startling concrete ratio or everyday mystery (*"1 liter = 1,109 km"*, *"Scroll HP, kok air menguap?"*).
2. **Context & Intuitive Hypothesis (5–20s):** Anchor in real competition/standards or state and test a common misconception (*"Dugaan pertama..."*).
3. **Mechanism + Unit-to-Physical-Object Analogy (20–65s):** Translate abstract physics/data units into tangible visual objects (e.g., `30 J/m = 3 marbles on a ruler`) backed by real primary-source citations.
4. **Everyday Relatable Benchmark (65–85s):** Contrast the technical result against the viewer's daily baseline (commuter motorbike, household electricity bill, Rupiah per session).
5. **Signature Thesis Closing (last 3s):** End every episode with a consistent 3–5 word brand tagline (e.g., *"Hitung dulu, baru hijau."*).

## 3. 30-Day Signal-First Trend Research & CRO (`last30days` + `marketingskills`)

- **Trend Mining**: Search Reddit, X, YouTube, and HN from the past 30 days; rank by real upvotes, comments, and recurring frustrations; extract exact audience phrasing.
- **Above-the-Fold Clarity**: State *what it is*, *who it's for*, and *concrete outcome* in one headline + adjacent proof + one unmistakable CTA.
- **Objection Handling**: Address cost, setup effort, and compatibility right next to the CTA.
