# YouTube & Instagram Content Growth Playbook

> Sub-module for `SCRIBE` covering YouTube packaging/retention/chapters/EDL/Shorts and Instagram caption/carousel/repurposing/analytics workflows.

---

## 1. YouTube Packaging, Retention & SEO

- **Title + Thumbnail Pairing**: Audit title & thumbnail together as one unit — keep titles < 60 chars (avoid mobile truncation), never duplicate thumbnail text inside the title, and replace vague teasers with concrete tension/specificity.
- **First 15s Hook Scoring**: Generate hook candidates across distinct curiosity/contrast/proof formulas, score for Topic Clarity + On-Target Curiosity, and mark spoken retention beats every 15–25s.
- **Audience Retention Diagnosis**: Read retention curves/CSV exports to locate exact drop spikes (slow intro, re-explaining title, flat visual transition, premature payoff) and prescribe structural fixes before pacing tweaks.
- **YouTube Chapters Validation**: Extract section timestamps from transcript and enforce YouTube's rendering rules (`00:00` start mandatory, minimum 3 chapters, each chapter >= 10s, concise scannable labels).
- **Edit Decision List (EDL) & Dead-Air Cuts**: Flag dead air, filler cues, stumbles, and retakes with exact timecodes so footage tightens before export.
- **Shorts Extraction & Outlier Spy**: Extract self-contained 30–55s moments from long-form transcripts; rank competitor videos by view-to-subscriber outlier multiplier to isolate repeatable topic + packaging formulas.
- **Search-Query-First SEO**: Build descriptions, first-2-lines hook, and tags around exact queries real viewers type rather than keyword-stuffed spam.

---

## 2. Instagram Captions, Carousels, Repurposing & Audits

- **Instagram Caption Linting**: Hook on Line 1 must survive the `... more` cutoff; follow with tight value body, ONE clear CTA (e.g., keyword DM or save), natural search keywords, and at most 3 targeted hashtags.
- **1080×1350 Carousel Architecture**: Slide 1 earns the swipe (clear outcome + visual tension); middle slides deliver 1 punchy idea per frame (`<= 25` words/slide); final slide lands a single action CTA.
- **1-to-Many Repurposing**: Turn 1 long asset (YouTube video, podcast, newsletter, client call) into a week of native Reels scripts + Carousels without sounding copy-pasted.
- **Content Post-Mortem Audit**: Sort past Reels/posts by watch time, saves, and shares-per-reach — double down on top outlier formats and kill consistent low-retention templates.
